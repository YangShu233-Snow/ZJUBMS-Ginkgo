> 线下计分小测，共2题。

- A researcher wants to test whether mean systolic blood pressure (SBP) differs among three groups of adults following different diets using one-way ANOVA:
    | Group        |    n | SBP (mmHg)              |
    | ------------ | ---: | ----------------------- |
    | A (Low-salt) |    5 | 118, 122, 119, 121, 120 |
    | B (Regular)  |    5 | 130, 128, 132, 129, 131 |
    | C (High-fat) |    5 | 135, 138, 134, 136, 137 |
    Please perform the test and draw a conclusion (alpha = 0.05, keep two decimals during your calculation).

  - 单因素方差分析：组均值 A=120.00，B=130.00，C=136.00，总均值 128.67；SSB=653.33，SSW=30.00，df=2,12，MSB=326.67，MSW=2.50，F=130.67；alpha=0.05 时 F 临界值约 3.89，F>临界值，拒绝 H0，可认为三组不同饮食者的平均收缩压差异有统计学意义。

- Please examine whether age (x) is linearly associated with systolic blood pressure (SBP, y). 
    | Patient | Age (x, years) | SBP (y, mmHg) |
    | ------- | -------------: | ------------: |
    | 1       |             20 |           118 |
    | 2       |             25 |           121 |
    | 3       |             30 |           127 |
    | 4       |             35 |           130 |
    | 5       |             40 |           135 |
    | 6       |             45 |           142 |
    (1) Please define your model ${y = bx + a}$. 
    (2) Please use F-test to determine if age is significantly associated with SBP (alpha = 0.05).
    
  - (1) 线性回归模型为 y = 0.943x + 98.19；(2) Sxx=437.5，Sxy=412.5，b=0.943，a=98.19，SST=394.83，SSR=388.93，SSE=5.90，F=263.46，df=1,4；alpha=0.05 时 F 临界值约 7.71，F>临界值，拒绝 H0，可认为年龄与收缩压存在显著线性关联。
