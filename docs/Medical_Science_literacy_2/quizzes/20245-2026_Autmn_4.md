## flashcards

> 线下计分小测，共2题。

- A study was conducted to investigate the effect of a new medication on systolic blood pressure. Blood pressure was measured for 8 patients before and after taking the medication: Participant 1 Pre 140 Post 130; Participant 2 Pre 150 Post 142; Participant 3 Pre 135 Post 128; Participant 4 Pre 160 Post 152; Participant 5 Pre 145 Post 138; Participant 6 Pre 155 Post 148; Participant 7 Pre 148 Post 142; Participant 8 Pre 142 Post 135. Please determine if the medication has a significant effect on reducing blood pressure at alpha = 0.05.
  - 采用配对样本 t 检验，差值 Pre-Post 为 10,8,7,8,7,7,6,7，均值 7.5，样本标准差约 1.195，标准误约 0.423，t≈17.75，df=7；单尾 alpha=0.05 时临界值约 1.895，t>临界值，拒绝 H0，可认为该药物显著降低收缩压。

- Suppose you want to test whether smoking status is associated with lung disease in a sample of 200 individuals. Table: Lung Disease 60, No Disease 40, Total 100 for Smoker; Lung Disease 30, No Disease 70, Total 100 for Non-smoker; Total Lung Disease 90, No Disease 110, Total 200. Please fill the following table with expected frequencies and perform a Chi-square test at alpha = 0.05 (keep two decimals during your calculation).
  - 期望频数：Smoker-Lung Disease=45.00，Smoker-No Disease=55.00，Non-smoker-Lung Disease=45.00，Non-smoker-No Disease=55.00；卡方统计量约 18.18，df=1，alpha=0.05 时临界值约 3.841，拒绝 H0，可认为吸烟状态与肺病存在关联。
