## flashcards

> 线下计分小测，共2题。

- A study was conducted to investigate the effect of a new medication on systolic blood pressure. Blood pressure was measured for 8 patients before and after taking the medication: Participant 1 Pre 140 Post 150; Participant 2 Pre 150 Post 142; Participant 3 Pre 135 Post 128; Participant 4 Pre 160 Post 180; Participant 5 Pre 145 Post 138; Participant 6 Pre 155 Post 148; Participant 7 Pre 148 Post 170; Participant 8 Pre 142 Post 135. Please determine if the medication has a significant effect on changing blood pressure at alpha = 0.01.
  - 采用配对样本 t 检验，差值 Post-Pre 为 10,-8,-7,20,-7,-7,22,-7，均值 2，样本标准差约 13.16，标准误约 4.65，t=0.43，df=7；双尾 alpha=0.01 时临界值约 3.499，|t|<临界值，不拒绝 H0，尚不能认为该药物对改变血压有显著影响。

- Suppose you want to test whether smoking status is associated with lung disease in a sample of 200 individuals. Table: Lung Disease 65, No Disease 40, Total 105 for Smoker; Lung Disease 25, No Disease 75, Total 100 for Non-smoker; Total Lung Disease 90, No Disease 115, Total 205. Please fill the following table with expected frequencies and perform a Chi-square test at alpha = 0.01 (keep two decimals during your calculation).
  - 期望频数：Smoker-Lung Disease=46.10，Smoker-No Disease=58.90，Non-smoker-Lung Disease=43.90，Non-smoker-No Disease=56.10；卡方统计量约 28.33，df=1，alpha=0.01 时临界值约 6.635，拒绝 H0，可认为吸烟状态与肺病存在关联。
